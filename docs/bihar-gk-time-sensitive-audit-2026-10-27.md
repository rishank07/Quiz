# Bihar GK time-sensitive data review — 27 October 2026

Scope: all 10 reading topics (1,522 existing bilingual fact cards). The Current Affairs page and Bihar Objective GK – 60 Sets are excluded. Their HTML and all their search records remain unchanged.

42 existing fact cards were revised across Introducing Bihar, Bihar Polity, Bihar Geography and Major Changes in Bihar Economy. Fact numbering, bookmark anchors, page routes and storage keys are unchanged.

## Reporting periods and evidence

- Officeholders and directory entries are checked as of the review date.
- Statistical reference years are preserved; a review date is not a new measurement year. IMD annual actual rainfall is for the completed calendar year 2025. The district rainfall ranking is explicitly retained as a **2024 historical table**, corroborated in Economic Survey 2024–25 summaries; no unverified 2025/2026 district ranking is claimed.
- ULB total 264 is checked against the SEC live directory; its 19/88/157 type split is corroborated by the LGD-derived Bharat Survey directory (25 September 2026 data). Government allotment notices sometimes count only functioning/grant-eligible bodies; these are not interchangeable with directory totals.
- The Panchayati Raj Department lists 8,053 functional Gram Panchayats. Other central reports count different sets of equivalent/onboarded bodies; these are not silently substituted.
- Patna High Court: 53 is the sanctioned strength including the Chief Justice, not a claim about judges currently in office.
- GSVA shares use constant (2011–12) prices, comparing 2020–21 with 2024–25; they are not employment shares.
- Kosi–Mechi: 2,10,516 hectares is **additional annual Kharif irrigation under the March 2025 Cabinet approval**, not the rounded total irrigation coverage mentioned in later replies. Target completion is not reported as actual completion.
- Bridge design length is distinguished from completed/open traffic status; no unsupported “longest operational bridge” rank is retained.
- IMC Gaya MoUs indicate proposed investment, not investment already received.
