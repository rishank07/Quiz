⚠ Exam Trap

Only six states have Legislative Councils; the remaining states are unicameral. Bihar has a Legislative Council. The Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir, Delhi and Puducherry have Legislative Assemblies.

💡 Quick Recall — Centre Parallel

The Vidhan Sabha corresponds to the Lok Sabha: it is the directly elected lower House and may be dissolved. The Vidhan Parishad corresponds to the Rajya Sabha: it is a continuing upper House, with one-third of its members retiring every two years.

⚠ Exam Trap

A Legislative Assembly normally has at least 60 members, but smaller States such as Sikkim, Goa and Mizoram have constitutionally permitted exceptions. Remember both the general rule and the exceptions.

✅ Qualifications for Membership
  • Indian Citizen
  • Min. Age → 25 years (Vidhan Sabha) · 30 years (Vidhan Parishad)
  • Must be an elector of relevant Legislative Assembly Constituency
⚠ Exam Trap

Minimum age: 25 years for the Vidhan Sabha and Lok Sabha; 30 years for the Vidhan Parishad and Rajya Sabha. The upper houses have the higher age requirement.

⚡ Powers of Legislative Assembly
  • Executive Powers → Controls State Government & Council of Ministers CoM is responsible to Vidhan Sabha, not Vidhan Parishad
  • Legislative Powers → Makes laws on: ✦ State List subjects ✦ Concurrent List subjects
  • Election-related Powers ✦ Election of President (MLAs participate) ✦ Election of Rajya Sabha members (MLAs vote)
  • Financial Powers ✦ Budget introduced only in Legislative Assembly ✦ Without Assembly's approval — no taxes, no money withdrawal

Parliament has the power to create or abolish a State Legislative Council. The process begins when the Legislative Assembly passes a resolution by special majority, after which Parliament acts by simple majority.

💡 Quick Recall — Trick

Composition check: 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/12 + 1/12 + 1/6 = 1. The Governor nominates one-sixth of the Legislative Council; unlike the Rajya Sabha, this is a fraction rather than a fixed number of 12.

⚠ Exam Trap

The President nominates a fixed number of 12 members to the Rajya Sabha, whereas the Governor nominates one-sixth of the members of a Legislative Council.

🎙️ Speaker & Deputy Speaker — Vidhan Sabha
  • Elected from among its own members
  • Tenure → Usually till the Assembly term
  • Resignation → Speaker resigns to Deputy Speaker · Vice versa
  • Removal → By Legislative Assembly · 14 days' prior notice
  • Speaker Absent → Deputy Speaker acts as Speaker
⚠ Exam Trap

Removal of a Legislative Assembly Speaker requires 14 days' notice. The Speaker resigns to the Deputy Speaker, not the Governor. The Speaker takes the oath as a member; there is no separate oath for the office of Speaker.

🪑 Chairman & Deputy Chairman — Vidhan Parishad
  • Elected from among the members of Vidhan Parishad
  • Chairman presides over meetings
  • If Chairman absent → Deputy Chairman presides
💡 Quick Recall — Centre vs State Parallel

At the Union level, the Vice-President is the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha and is not its member. At the State level, the Legislative Council elects its Chairman from among its own members.

⚠ Exam Trap

The Vice-President of India, a non-member, chairs the Rajya Sabha. A Legislative Council elects its Chairman from among its members.

💰 Salary & Allowances
  • Salaries & Allowances → Determined by State Legislature Art 195
  • Minimum 2 sessions must be held every year
💡 Quick Recall

Parliament determines the salaries of MPs, while each State Legislature determines the salaries of its MLAs and MLCs.

📜 Bills in State Legislature
🟢 Ordinary Bills
  • Can be introduced in either House (VS or VP)
  • Final authority rests with Legislative Assembly
  • VP can delay:
    → 3 months on 1st instance
    → 1 additional month on re-passage
💛 Money Bills
  • Introduced only in Legislative Assembly
  • VP can only withhold for 14 days
  • No amendment power for VP
  • Budget also only in Legislative Assembly
⚠ Exam Trap

There is no provision for a joint sitting at the State level. A Legislative Council may delay an ordinary Bill for a maximum of four months in two stages, but may retain a Money Bill for only 14 days. The Speaker of the Legislative Assembly decides whether a Bill is a Money Bill.

💡 Quick Recall — Centre vs State Bills

Centre: RS Money Bill delay = 14 days. State: VP Money Bill delay = 14 days (same!). Centre: Ordinary bill RS = indefinite hold → Joint Sitting. State: VP = max 4 months (3+1), no joint sitting.

🛡️ Privileges of Members (Art. 194)
🔵 Collective Privileges 🟢 Individual Privileges
Right to hold secret sessions Immunity from civil arrest
Authority to arrest under simple warrant Proceedings of House cannot be challenged in Court
Punishment for Contempt of House (Member / Non-Member) Freedom from being summoned as witness
Right to publish or restrict proceedings Freedom of Speech in Legislature
⚠ Exam Trap

Members enjoy limited immunity from arrest in civil cases, but not in criminal cases, during the session and for 40 days before and after it. Legislative freedom of speech is protected from court proceedings, subject to constitutional rules.

💡 Quick Recall

Article 194 concerns the privileges of State Legislatures, while Article 105 concerns those of Parliament. Both provide broadly corresponding individual and collective privileges.

📅 Sessions of State Legislature
  • Minimum 2 sessions must be held every year
  • Prorogation → By the Governor
  • Adjournment → By the Presiding Officer (Speaker/Chairman)
  • Dissolution → By the Governor (Only for Vidhan Sabha)
When can Governor dissolve Vidhan Sabha?
  • When tenure is over (5 years)
  • Or when government fails to prove majority in the House
⚠ Exam Trap

Prorogation ends a session and is ordered by the Governor; adjournment temporarily suspends a sitting and is ordered by the presiding officer. Only the Legislative Assembly can be dissolved; the Legislative Council is permanent.

💡 Quick Recall

At the Union level, the President summons and prorogues Parliament while the presiding officer adjourns a sitting. At the State level, the Governor summons and prorogues the Legislature while its presiding officer adjourns a sitting.
