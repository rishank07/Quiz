Analog Computer
  • Result ko analog signals me express karta hai.
  • Pressure, temperature, length jaise physical quantities ko measure karne me use.
  • Scientific aur engineering field me zyada use.
  • Example: Thermometer.
Digital Computer
  • Number system / binary numbers par work karta hai.
  • Source ke anusaar, correct data dene par correct result deta hai.
  • Examples: Calculator, Digital Watch.
Hybrid Computer
  • Digital aur analog dono form me result express karta hai.
  • Example: Petrol pump par oil/fuel dispensing machine.
⚡ One-Line Difference
Analog = continuous physical quantities • Digital = binary/number system • Hybrid = Analog + Digital
🚀 Super Computer
  • Speed Unit: FLOPS — source expands it as “Floting Point Operation Per Second”.
  • Super computer banane wali company: C-DAC — “Centre for Development Advance Computing” as printed in source.
  • भारत का सबसे पहला सुपर कम्प्यूटर: PARAM 8000.
  • Source me duniya ka sabse tez computer: Fortior.
  • Source me भारत का वर्तमान सबसे तेज सुपर कम्प्यूटर: Airawat — 13170 Teraflop.
  • Use: scientific purposes.
  • Source line: Cary Ist duniya ka pehla super computer.
📘 Source Fidelity
Names, spellings aur “current fastest” jaise statements ko PDF me jis tarah diya gaya hai, usi tarah preserve kiya gaya hai; is chapter me outside web-verification add nahi ki gayi.
⚠ Exam Trap

⚠ High-Confusion Pairs
  • Analog measures/represents physical quantities; Digital works on binary/number system.
  • Hybrid combines analog + digital characteristics.
  • Super and Mainframe are both powerful, but source separately treats supercomputer for scientific purposes and mainframe for large-scale processing/multitasking.
  • Mini ≠ Micro: micro uses a microprocessor and is listed as PC category.
