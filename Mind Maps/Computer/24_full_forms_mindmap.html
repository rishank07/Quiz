Source-Specific Traps
VGA appears twice in the PDF: “Video Graphics Array” and “Video/Visual Graphic Adapter.”
MB is given as “Motherboard/ Megabyte.” HPC is given as “Handheld Personal Computer/High Performance Computer.”
PDU is given as “Protocol Data Unit / Power Distribution Unit.” SAM is given as “Software Asset Management/Sequential Access Method.”
The source text for ISO appears as “International rganization for Standardization”; it has been retained as printed/extracted rather than silently repaired.
