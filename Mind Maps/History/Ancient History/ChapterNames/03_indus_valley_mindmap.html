📐 Construction Features ▼
  • Grid Pattern: Streets intersected at right angles (90°)
  • Construction Material: Baked and sun-dried bricks; Dholavira used stone prominently
  • Drainage System: Main/defining feature of the civilization
  • House Windows: Houses had windows
  • Doors: Many entrances protected privacy; arrangements varied
  • Exception: Lothal — door arrangements were not a universal exception
Granaries
  • Great Granary: Found at Mohenjo-daro (function debated)
  • Harappa Granaries: Twelve foundation blocks in two rows of six; granary function debated
🌾 Agriculture Crop remains vary by site ▼
  • Major Crops: Wheat, barley, pulses and sesame; cotton for fibre; millets at some Gujarat sites
  • Rice: Found at Rangpur and Lothal
  • Ploughed field evidence: Kalibangan
  • Irrigation: Canal traces at Shortughai; wells and reservoirs were other water sources
  • Dholavira: Unique water harnessing — dams/reservoirs
  • Horse: Debated — bones reported from Surkotada (Gujarat)

⚠ TRAP 01 — Discoverer vs Excavator
Dayaram Sahni: Harappa excavations, 1921.
R. D. Banerjee: Mohenjo-daro investigations, 1922.
John Marshall: ASI director; announced the recognised civilisation in 1924. The mounds were known earlier.
⚠ TRAP 02 — Largest Site
Rakhigarhi, Haryana, is widely recognised as the largest known Harappan settlement by geographical spread. Sizes of Mohenjo-daro, Harappa and Dholavira depend on surveyed boundaries and phases; rigid second/third/fourth ranks are unsafe.
⚠ TRAP 03 — Only City Without Citadel
Chanhudaro is commonly described as lacking a separate citadel. Dholavira has citadel, middle town and lower town; settlement plans varied across sites.
⚠ TRAP 04 — Doors Direction
Many Mohenjo-daro entrances protected privacy by avoiding direct views into interiors. Door placement varied across sites and buildings; Lothal is not a universal exception rule.
⚠ TRAP 05 — Rice Sites
Rangpur: reported rice-husk impressions; Lothal: reported rice finds need scrutiny. Koldihwa is Neolithic and associated with early rice remains; this does not establish a worldwide first.
⚠ TRAP 06 — Granaries
The “granary” labels at Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are interpretations; grain-storage functions are debated.
Harappa: twelve foundation blocks in two rows of six.
⚠ TRAP 07 — Lothal Basin Interpretation
Lothal: large brick-lined basin commonly interpreted as a dockyard, with its function also debated. “Manchester of IVC” is a modern exam-reference nickname. S. R. Rao excavated Lothal and Rangpur.
⚠ TRAP 08 — First Site in Independent India
Ropar (Rupnagar) = First IVC site excavated in independent India (1953, Y. D. Sharma)[cite: 12]
Harappa and Mohenjo-daro were excavated in 1921 and 1922, respectively, in British India.[cite: 12]
⚠ TRAP 09 — Cotton
Woven cotton evidence is associated with Mohenjo-daro; very early cotton-use evidence also comes from Mehrgarh. The Greek term “Sindon” does not establish a Harappan etymology.
