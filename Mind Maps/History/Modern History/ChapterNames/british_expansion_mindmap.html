⚠️ Trap #1 — Plassey vs Buxar commander
Plassey (1757): Robert Clive's was. Buxar (1764): Hector Munro's was. They are different — Robert Clive Buxar in not give!
⚠️ Trap #2 — Black Hole numbers
Examinations usually use Holwell's figures: 146 imprisoned and 23 survivors. Modern historians give different estimates—about 64 imprisoned and 43 deaths—but BPSC/BSSC questions generally expect Holwell's figures.
⚠️ Trap #3 — Mir Jafar twice Nawab became
Mir Jafar twice Bengal's Nawab was: 1757–1760 and then 1763–1765. Beech in Mir Qasim was (1760–1763). Sequence remember.
⚠️ Trap #4 — Carnatic Wars's treaties
1st → Aix-la-Chapelle 1748 (status quo) 2nd → Pondicherry 1755 (Dupleix recall) 3rd → Paris 1763 (French power end)
⚠️ Trap #5 — Bombay how acquired?
Bombay was not conquered; Charles II received it as dowry in 1661 through his marriage to the Portuguese princess Catherine of Braganza.
⚠️ Trap #6 — Anglo-Mysore Wars's GGs
1st: No GG mentioned
2nd: Warren Hastings — Mangalore 1784
3rd: Cornwallis — Seringapatam 1792
4th: Wellesley — Tipu killed 1799
⚠️ Trap #7 — Wandiwash (1760) commanders
British: Eyre Coote (won). French: Count de Lally (lost). This 3rd Carnatic War was in — exact names are frequently asked.
⚠️ Trap #8 — Anglo-Afghan 1st War result
The First Afghan War (1839–1842) was disastrous for the British, who had to retreat from Kabul. Sindh was annexed in 1843; it was a different event from the same broad period.
