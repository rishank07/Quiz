East India Company's rule ended.

Political Causes

  • Doctrine of Lapse (Lord Dalhousie): son not → state British le taking. Satara (1848), Sambalpur (1849), Jhansi (1853).
  • Subsidiary Alliance under Lord Wellesley: Indian rulers had to maintain a British army and follow British wishes.

Economic Causes

  • Permanent Settlement: High land revenue demands impoverished the peasants.
  • Ryotwari System: directly peasants from tax → burden more.
  • Mahalwari System: villages with tax settlement → then also distressed.

Social Causes

  • Christian missionaries were very active, creating a fear that the entire population would be converted to Christianity.
  • Racial discrimination: The British treated Indians badly.
  • Social reforms: Measures against Sati, child marriage and female infanticide offended orthodox sections of society.

Immediate Cause

  • Enfield Rifle arrived — cartridge mouth from bite had to was.
  • Rumour: Cartridge in Cow fat (Hindu for sin) + Pig fat (Muslim for haram).
  • sepoys very angry arrived in → Spark of Revolt.

Governance After the Revolt — 1858 Changes
Government of India Act, 1858 passed.
The East India Company's rule, which had existed since 1600, ended.
British Crown (Queen Victoria) supreme authority became.
The designation of Governor-General was changed to Viceroy.
Lord Canning = First Viceroy of India.

Trap #2 — Revolt when began?
Mangal Pandey incident: 29 March 1857 at Barrackpore. The revolt formally began on 10 May at Meerut, and the rebels reached Delhi on 11 May. These are three different dates.
Trap #3 — Real command in Delhi who was?
Bahadur Shah Zafar only symbolic leader was. Real military command = General Bakht Khan. They are different.
Trap #4 — Do not confuse the historians' interpretations
Savarkar = "War of Independence" (before) R.C. Majumdar = "Neither first, nor national, nor war of independence" Disraeli = "National Revolt" (British MP was!) T.R. Holmes = "Civilisation vs Barbarism"
Trap #5 — Jhansi waali quote
British commander Hugh Rose described Rani Lakshmibai as "the only man among the rebels". This exact attribution is frequently asked in examinations.

Trap #7 — Revolt when ended?
