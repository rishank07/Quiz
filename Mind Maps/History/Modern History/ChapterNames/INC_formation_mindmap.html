28 Oct 1851

05
Indian League
Sep 1875
Founder: Shishir Kumar Ghosh (Editor, Amrit Bazar journal)
Temp President: Shambhu Chandra Mukherjee

26 Jul 1876
Place: Albert Hall, Calcutta
Founders: Surendranath Banerjee + Anand Mohan Bose

Indian National Congress — Establishment
28 December 1885 | Bombay
📅
Date: 28th December 1885.
📍
Place: Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College, Bombay (near Gowalia Tank).
👤
Founder: Allan Octavian Hume (A.O. Hume) — British Civil Servant.
👑
First President: Womesh Chandra Bonnerjee (W.C. Bonnerjee).
🏛️
Viceroy at that time: Lord Dufferin.
First Session Stats
👥
Total Delegates: 72. Secretary: A.O. Hume (Organising Secretary).
🔢
Breakdown: Hindus (majority — 54), Muslims: 2, Others: Parsis, Jains, Christians.
📊
Region-wise: Bombay: 38 (max) → Madras: 21 → UP/Awadh: 7 → Bengal: 3 → Punjab: 3.
3 Theories of INC Formation
🛡️
Safety Valve Theory — A. O. Hume: A platform to channel Indian discontent and prevent another revolt like that of 1857.
⚡
Lightning Conductor Theory — Gopal Krishna Gokhale: Indians used Hume so that the British would not suppress the Congress at its inception.
🕵️
Conspiracy Theory — Rajani Palme Dutt (R.P. Dutt): It was a British conspiracy.
28 Dec 1885 = INC founded A.O. Hume = Founder (British) W.C. Bonnerjee = 1st President Lord Dufferin = Viceroy
1st Session 1885 Bombay
President: Womesh Chandra Bonnerjee | Secretary: A.O. Hume
72 delegates. Build coordination + National Unity. Bombay sent the largest contingent of 38 delegates.
2nd Session 1886 Calcutta
President: Dadabhai Naoroji
Key Fact: First Parsi President. Members: 434 (grew from 72).
3rd Session 1887 Madras
President: Syed Badruddin Tyabji
Key Fact: First Muslim President of Congress. "Subject Determination Committee" was formed.
4th Session 1888 Prayagraj (Allahabad)
President: George Yule
Key Fact: First English/British President. Lala Lajpat Rai participated for the first time in this session.
12th Session 1896 Calcutta
President: Rahimtulla Sayani
🎵 "Vande Mataram" was sung for the first time!Composer: Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (Anandamath, 1882). Singer: Rabindranath Tagore.
22nd Session 1906 Calcutta
President: Dadabhai Naoroji
The word "Swaraj" was used for the first time. 4 Resolutions passed:1. Swaraj (Self-rule) as goal. 2. Boycott Movement. 3. Swacountryi. 4. National Education.
23rd Session 1907 Surat
President: Rash Bihari Ghosh
💥 FIRST SPLIT OF THE CONGRESS — Surat Split! Moderates vs Extremists. Reason: The Extremists wanted the four resolutions of 1906 to be implemented throughout India, while the Moderates wanted to restrict them to Bengal.
26th Session 1911 Calcutta
President: Pandit Bishan Narayan Dhar
🎵 "Jana Gana Mana" (the National Anthem) was sung for the first time!Composer: Rabindranath Tagore.
31st Session 1916 Lucknow
President: Ambika Charan Majumdar
1. Reconciliation: Extremists and Moderates reunited. 2. Lucknow Pact: Agreement between the Congress and the Muslim League.
32nd Session 1917 Calcutta
President: Annie Besant
⭐ First Female President of Congress (videshi / foreign). Note: First Indian Female = Sarojini Naidu (1925).
39th Session 1924 Belgaum (Karnataka)
President: Mahatma Gandhi
⭐ Gandhi presided over the Congress only once — at this session.
44th Session 1929 Lahore (Ravi river on the bank of)
President: Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru
⭐ "Purna Swaraj" resolution passed (Complete Independence). Decided: 26 January 1930 the first Independence Day would be observed.
45th Session 1931 Karachi
President: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Resolution: "Fundamental Rights" + "National Economic Programme" passed.

Memory Tricks
🔥 Surat Split 1907
"Surat in Congress's Surat broke down." (Split was completed).
🍀 Lucknow Pact 1916
Lucknow brought together the Moderates, Extremists and the Muslim League.
🌿 Gandhi 1924 Belgaum
Gandhi presided only once—at Belgaum in 1924. Remember: 1924 was his sole presidency.
👩 Females — 2 are, both different
1917: Annie Besant (Foreign — British). 1925: Sarojini Naidu (Indian — Indian). eight years's gap.
⚠️ Trap #1 — 1st Female vs 1st Indian Female President
First Female President = Annie Besant (1917, British) — Foreign was.First Indian Female President = Sarojini Naidu (1925, Kanpur) — Indian. eight years's gap.
⚠️ Trap #2 — Vande Mataram composer vs singer
Composer (Writer): Bankim Chandra Chatterjee (in novel Anandamath, 1882).First Singer (1896 INC session): Rabindranath Tagore. both different roles!
⚠️ Trap #3 — Jana Gana Mana vs Vande Mataram sessions
Vande Mataram = 12th Session, Calcutta, 1896 (presided over by Sayani). Jana Gana Mana = 26th Session, Calcutta, 1911 (presided over by Bishan Narayan Dhar). Both were held in Calcutta, but in different years.
⚠️ Trap #4 — Surat Split (1907)'s president
Surat Split 1907's President = Rash Bihari Ghosh. Tilak or Lal-Bal-Pal not — that leaders the, president not. only president remember.
⚠️ Trap #5 — First Organized Political Association
India's First Organized Political Association = Landholders' Society (1838), founder: Dwarkanath Tagore. Indian National Association (1876) do not confuse them.
⚠️ Trap #6 — Dadabhai Naoroji = "Swaraj" first use
Word "Swaraj" for the first time Dadabhai Naoroji used — 22nd Session (1906) in Calcutta, when that President the. Gokhale or Tilak not.
⚠️ Trap #7 — Lightning Conductor = Gokhale, Safety Valve = Hume
Safety Valve Theory = A.O. Hume gave.Lightning Conductor Theory = Gopal Krishna Gokhale gave.Conspiracy Theory = R.P. Dutt gave. All three are different!
⚠️ Trap #8 — Indian National Association merged INC in
Indian National Association (1876, Surendranath Banerjee) — Most Important Pre-Congress org — 1886 in INC merged into. This first All-India political platform was.
⚠️ Trap #9 — Muslim League founder vs 1st President
Muslim League (1906, Dhaka):Initiative: Nawab Salimullah of Dhaka.First President: Aga Khan.HQ: Lucknow. All three are different — do not mix them up.
⚠️ Trap #10 — Lucknow Session 1916 in 2 events
31st Session (1916, Lucknow) in 2 events: (1) Moderates + Extremists patch-up. (2) Lucknow Pact — Congress + Muslim League agreement. President: Ambika Charan Majumdar (Tilak not).
