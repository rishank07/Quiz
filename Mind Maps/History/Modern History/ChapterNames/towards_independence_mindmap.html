⚠️ Trap: Liaqat Ali Khan = Finance
Liaquat Ali Khan of the Muslim League received the Finance portfolio. Giving such a powerful ministry to a League member was unusual and is frequently tested.
⚠️ Trap #1 — Gandhi's jail = Agha Khan Palace (not Yerwada)
During the Quit India Movement of 1942, Gandhi was detained at the Aga Khan Palace in Pune[cite: 6]. He had been held in Yerwada Jail during the Civil Disobedience Movement of 1930[cite: 6]. Do not confuse the two.
⚠️ Trap #2 — Individual Satyagraha sequence
1st = Vinoba Bhave, 2nd = Jawaharlal Nehru, 3rd = Brahma Dutt[cite: 6]. in state exams 3rd who was is frequently asked — Brahma Dutt.
⚠️ Trap #3 — INA Founder vs Supreme Commander
Founder: Captain Mohan Singh (1942)[cite: 6]. Supreme Commander: Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose (July 1943 from)[cite: 6]. They are different!
⚠️ Trap #4 — Pakistan name vs idea
Idea of a separate Muslim state: Muhammad Iqbal at the Allahabad Session in 1930[cite: 6]. Name "Pakistan": Choudhary Rahmat Ali in 1933[cite: 6]. Official demand: Jinnah at the Lahore Session in 1940[cite: 6]. Remember the three different persons and dates.
⚠️ Trap #5 — Liaqat Ali Khan = Finance (ML's member)
In the Interim Government, the Finance portfolio went to Liaquat Ali Khan, a member of the Muslim League, not the Congress[cite: 6].
⚠️ Trap #6 — Gowalia Tank = August revolution Maidan
Quit India Movement launch place = Gowalia Tank Maidan, Mumbai = today August revolution Maidan called are[cite: 6]. both same place's different name are.
⚠️ Trap #7 — Cabinet Mission members: "LCA"
Cabinet Mission: Pethick-Lawrence, Stafford Cripps and A. V. Alexander[cite: 6]. Cripps served on both the 1942 Cripps Mission and the 1946 Cabinet Mission; do not confuse the two.
⚠️ Trap #8 — Independence Act dates
The bill was introduced on 4 July 1947[cite: 6], received royal assent on 18 July[cite: 6], and independence followed on 15 August[cite: 6]. These are three different dates.
⚠️ Trap #9 — Red Fort Trial I lawyer
At the first Red Fort Trial in 1945, Bhulabhai Desai led the defence team[cite: 6]; Nehru also participated. The three accused were Dhillon, Prem Sehgal and Shahnawaz Khan[cite: 6].
⚠️ Trap #10 — Satara = longest parallel govt
Parallel governments: Ballia was the first[cite: 6]; Satara lasted the longest, until 1946[cite: 6]; and Tamluk organised the Vidyut Vahini and cyclone relief[cite: 6]. Remember these separate facts.
