Revolutionary Activities — Origin & Spread
📍
Beginning: Shuruwat Maharashtra se hui — first centre of Nationalist Revolutionaries.
🗺️
Spread: Maximum spread Bengal, Maharashtra, aur Punjab mein dekhne ko mila.
Vyayam Mandal (1896–97, Poona)
👥
Founders: Chapekar Brothers — Damodar Hari Chapekar & Balkrishna Hari Chapekar.
🎯
Objective: Youth ko gymnastics aur arms training (hathiyar chalana) sikha kar sarkar ka virodh karna.
🔫
Assassination: Plague Commissioner Mr. Rand aur Lieutenant Ayerst — by Chapekar Brothers, 22nd June 1897. One of the earliest significant political assassinations of British officials in India.
Mitra Mela & Abhinav Bharat (Maharashtra)
📅
Mitra Mela: Established 1899 CE by Veer Savarkar (Vinayak Damodar Savarkar).
🔀
Evolution: 1904 mein Mitra Mela ka naam badalkar 'Abhinav Bharat' kar diya gaya — founders Vinayak Damodar Savarkar & Ganesh Damodar Savarkar.
🇮🇹
Inspiration: Mazzini ki 'Young Italy' movement se inspired thi.

Home Rule Movement
🇮🇪
Inspiration: Home Rule League in Ireland — Leader in Ireland: Redmond.
🔀
India mein 2 Leagues thi: Tilak's League (April 1916) & Annie Besant's League (September 1916).
A. Tilak's Home Rule League
Established: 28th April 1916.
Headquarters: Pune (Poona).
First President: Joseph Baptista.
First Secretary: N.C. Kelkar.
Objective: British Empire ke under rehte hue Responsible Government haasil karna.
Area: Karnataka, Central Provinces (CP), Berar, Maharashtra (except Bombay City).
B. Annie Besant's All India Home Rule League
Established: September 1916.
Headquarters: Madras (Adyar).
Founder: Annie Besant. Secretary: George Arundale.
Objective: Self-Government (Swashasan).
Area: Rest of India — includes Bombay City.
Key Members: Subramania Iyer, Motilal Nehru, Tej Bahadur Sapru.
👤
Profile: Annie Besant
Exam Favorite Facts
Key Point Details
Origin Irish Woman (Ireland ki thi)
Newspapers Commonweal (Weekly), New India (Daily)
Congress Role First Female President of INC in 1917 (Calcutta Session)
Tilak's League: Pune HQ, no Bombay City Besant's League: Madras HQ, includes Bombay City Delhi Durbar 1911 = announcement; capital official 1912 Annie Besant = New India; Bipin Pal = Bande Mataram
⚠️ Trap #1 — Partition of Bengal: 3 dates
Announced: 19 July 1905. Effective: 16 October 1905. Cancelled: 1911 (Delhi Durbar, Lord Hardinge II). Teeno alag dates hain.
⚠️ Trap #2 — 16 October 1905 ka double reaction
Same din — Day of Mourning (shok) manaya gaya AND Tagore ke kehne par Rakhi Day (unity) bhi manaya gaya. Dono ek hi tareekh ko hue.
⚠️ Trap #3 — Mitra Mela vs Abhinav Bharat
Mitra Mela (1899) hi renamed hokar Abhinav Bharat (1904) bani — Savarkar Brothers ne. Ye 2 alag organizations nahi, ek hi hai naam badla.
⚠️ Trap #4 — Anushilan Samiti vs Dhaka Anushilan Samiti
Anushilan Samiti (1902, Calcutta) = P. Mitra ne banayi. Dhaka Anushilan Samiti (1905, Dhaka) = Pulin Das ne banayi (P. Mitra se inspired, but separate branch with ~500 sub-branches). Founders confuse mat karo.
⚠️ Trap #5 — Muzaffarpur Bomb Case: Target vs Victim
Asal target tha Judge D.H. Kingsford, lekin bomb galti se Kennedy ki gaadi par gira — Kingsford bach gaya. Khudiram Bose ko phaansi hui, Prafulla Chaki ne khud ko goli maar li.
⚠️ Trap #6 — Nasik Conspiracy vs Alipore Conspiracy
Nasik Conspiracy (1909): Jackson ki hatya — Anant Laxman Kanhere, Savarkar involved. Alipore Conspiracy (1908): Muzaffarpur bomb ke baad ke arrests — Aurobindo Ghosh acquitted, Kanailal Dutt/Satyendranath Bose ne approver Narendra Gosain ko jail mein maara. Dono cases alag hain.
⚠️ Trap #7 — Tilak's League vs Annie Besant's League (Bombay City)
Tilak's Home Rule League (Pune HQ) ka area Maharashtra tha EXCEPT Bombay City. Bombay City Annie Besant ke All India Home Rule League (Madras HQ) ke area mein aata tha. Yeh point exam mein bar-bar poocha jaata hai.
⚠️ Trap #8 — Delhi Durbar announcement vs official capital shift
Capital shift ki ghoshna (announcement) Delhi Durbar mein 1911 mein hui, par Delhi official capital 1912 mein bani. Ek hi saal mat samajhna.
⚠️ Trap #9 — India House (London) vs Paris India Society
India Home Rule Society/India House (London, 1905) = Shyamji Krishna Varma, magazine 'The Indian Sociologist', Madan Lal Dhingra ne yahin se inspire hokar Curzon Wyllie ko maara. Paris India Society (1905) = Madam Cama, S.R. Rana, M.G. Godrej — Stuttgart 1907 mein Tiranga fehraya. Do alag countries, alag founders.
⚠️ Trap #10 — Ghadar Party: Founder vs President
Ghadar Party (1913, San Francisco) ke Founder the Lala Hardayal, lekin President the Sohan Singh Bhakna. Founder aur President alag log the — exam mein swap karake poochha jaata hai.
