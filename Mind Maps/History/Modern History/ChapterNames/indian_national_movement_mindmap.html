⚠️ Trap #1 — Partition of Bengal: 3 dates
The Partition of Bengal was announced on 19 July 1905, took effect on 16 October 1905, and was annulled in 1911 at the Delhi Durbar under Lord Hardinge II. These are three different dates.
⚠️ Trap #2 — 16 October 1905's double reaction
On the same date, a Day of Mourning and Tagore's Rakhi Day for unity were both observed.
⚠️ Trap #3 — Mitra Mela vs Abhinav Bharat
Mitra Mela (1899), founded by the Savarkar brothers, was renamed Abhinav Bharat in 1904. These were not two different organisations; only the name changed.
⚠️ Trap #4 — Anushilan Samiti vs Dhaka Anushilan Samiti
Anushilan Samiti (Calcutta, 1902) was founded by P. Mitra. Dhaka Anushilan Samiti (Dhaka, 1905) was founded by Pulin Das; it was inspired by P. Mitra but became a separate branch with about 500 sub-branches. Do not confuse the founders.
⚠️ Trap #5 — Muzaffarpur Bomb Case: Target vs Victim
The intended target was Judge D. H. Kingsford, but the bomb mistakenly struck the Kennedys' carriage and Kingsford survived. Khudiram Bose was executed; Prafulla Chaki shot himself.
⚠️ Trap #6 — Nasik Conspiracy vs Alipore Conspiracy
Nasik Conspiracy (1909): Jackson was assassinated by Anant Laxman Kanhere; Savarkar was implicated. Alipore Conspiracy (1908): Arrests followed the Muzaffarpur bombing; Aurobindo Ghosh was acquitted, while Kanailal Dutt and Satyendranath Bose killed approver Narendra Gosain in jail. These were separate cases.
⚠️ Trap #7 — Tilak's League vs Annie Besant's League (Bombay City)
Tilak's Home Rule League, headquartered at Pune, covered Maharashtra except Bombay City. Bombay City belonged to the area of Annie Besant's All India Home Rule League, headquartered at Madras. This distinction is frequently tested.
⚠️ Trap #8 — Delhi Durbar announcement vs official capital shift
The transfer of the capital was announced at the Delhi Durbar in 1911, while Delhi officially became the capital in 1912. Do not treat them as the same year.
⚠️ Trap #9 — India House (London) vs Paris India Society
India Home Rule Society/India House (London, 1905) = Shyamji Krishna Varma, magazine 'The Indian Sociologist', Madan Lal Dhingra here from inspire and became Curzon Wyllie killed. Paris India Society (1905) = Madam Cama, S.R. Rana, M.G. Godrej — Stuttgart 1907 in hoisted the tricolour. Do different countries, different founders.
⚠️ Trap #10 — Ghadar Party: Founder vs President
Ghadar Party (1913, San Francisco)'s Founder the Lala Hardayal, but President the Sohan Singh Bhakna. Founder and President different people the — in exams swap by is frequently asked.
