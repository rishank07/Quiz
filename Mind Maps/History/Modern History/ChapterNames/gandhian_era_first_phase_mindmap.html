Three Early Satyagrahas — 1917–1918
Champaran Satyagraha
1917 | Bihar
Nature: First Civil Disobedience (First Civil Disobedience)
Who invited him? Rajkumar Shukla (Lucknow Session 1916 in united)
Team: Rajendra Prasad, J.B. Kripalani, Mahadev Desai, Anugrah Narayan Sinha
Result: Champaran Agrarian Act (1918). the Tinkathia System was abolished (3/20th land on indigo cultivation).Title "Mahatma": Rabindranath Tagore gave it after the success.Opposition: N.G. Ranga opposed.
Ahmedabad Mill Workers
1918 | Ahmedabad
Mudda (Issue): Plague Bonus
Weapon: First Hunger Strike (the first hunger strike)
Mill Owner: Ambalal Sarabhai
Supporter: Anusuya Sarabhai (Ambalal's sister — supported Gandhi)
Result: The workers received a 35% bonus.
Kheda Satyagraha
1918 | Gujarat
Significance: Gandhi's First Non-Cooperation Experiment (precursor to NCM)
Issue: The government refused to waive revenue despite crop failure. Under the rule, revenue was to be waived when the crop yield fell below 25%.
Associates: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (invited Gandhi), Narahari Parikh, Mohanlal Pandya, Ravi Shankar Vyas
Significance: the first experiment in revenue non-payment.
Champaran 1917 = 1st Civil Disobedience Ahmedabad 1918 = 1st Hunger Strike Kheda 1918 = 1st Non-cooperation experiment "Mahatma" title = Rabindranath Tagore (post-Champaran)

Rowlatt Act — 1919
Official Name: Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act, 1919
📌
The committee was formed in 1917 under the chairmanship of Sidney Rowlatt.
🖤
Called: "Black Act" (Black Act).
📢
Slogan: "No argument, No lawyer, No Appeal" — without lawyer, without appeal, without argument.
🎯
Objective: suppress revolutionary activities without trial.

Chauri-Chaura Incident & NCM Suspension
The Incident
📅
Date: 4th February 1922.
📍
Place: Chauri-Chaura, Gorakhpur, Uttar province.
👤
Key local figure: Bhagwan Ahir. A crowd gathered to protest against his beating.
🔥
Incident: A clash occurred between the police and local people, after which the crowd set the police station on fire.
💀
Result: 22 Policemen were burned alive (died).
Suspension of NCM
📅
Announcement Date: 12th February 1922.
📍
Place: Bardoli (Gujarat) → called "Bardoli Resolution."
⚖️
Reason: The violence at Chauri Chaura; Gandhi strongly opposed violence.
Consequences for Gandhi Ji
⛓️
Arrested: 10th March 1922.
⚖️
Sentenced: 18th March 1922 — 6 years's sentence.
🏥
Release: He was released in February 1924 because of ill health.

⚠️ Trap #1 — Pravasi Bharatiya Divas = 9 January
Gandhi returned to India 9 January 1915 [cite: 3]. On the same day Pravasi Bharatiya Divas is observed[cite: 3]. South Africa from return was — for the first time not arrived the[cite: 3].
⚠️ Trap #2 — "Mahatma" title kisgave?
Rabindranath Tagore Champaran Satyagraha's success after "Mahatma" title gave[cite: 3]. Gokhale not — that Gandhi's political guru the[cite: 3].
⚠️ Trap #3 — Indian Women's Association = Annie Besant, Gandhi not
The Women's Indian Association was founded in 1917 by Annie Besant and Margaret Cousins, not Gandhi. The year coincides with the Champaran Satyagraha and can cause confusion.
⚠️ Trap #4 — Shankaran Nair — most important MCQ
Jallianwala Bagh after Shankaran Nair Viceroy's Executive Council resigned from [cite: 3]. Tagore Knighthood returned, Gandhi Kaiser-i-Hind returned — separate reactions remember[cite: 3].
⚠️ Trap #5 — C.R. Das before oppose, then support
C. R. Das opposed the Non-Cooperation proposal at the Calcutta Session of 1920[cite: 3], but personally moved the supporting resolution at the Nagpur Session later that year[cite: 3]. His role differed between the two sessions.
⚠️ Trap #6 — "Himalayan Blunder" context
"Himalayan Blunder" was Gandhi's expression in the context of withdrawing the Non-Cooperation Movement after Chauri Chaura[cite: 3]. Subhas Chandra Bose called the withdrawal a "National Calamity"[cite: 3].
⚠️ Trap #7 — NCM start date = Tilak death date
The Non-Cooperation Movement began on 1 August 1920, the same morning that Bal Gangadhar Tilak died[cite: 3]. This coincidence is frequently asked in examinations.
⚠️ Trap #8 — Rowlatt Act resignations
Madan Mohan Malaviya, Mazharul Haq and Mohammad Ali Jinnah resigned from the Central Legislature in protest against the Rowlatt Act[cite: 3], not from the Viceroy's Executive Council.
⚠️ Trap #9 — Khilafat end = Mustafa Kemal Pasha 1924
Khilafat Movement 1919 began in — 1924 ended in when Mustafa Kemal Pasha Turkey in reforms introduced (Caliphate abolish's)[cite: 3]. Turkish reforms = Khilafat's end.
⚠️ Trap #10 — Who invited Gandhi to Champaran?
Rajkumar Shukla invited Gandhi to Champaran after meeting him at the Lucknow Session of 1916[cite: 3]. Gandhi's team included Rajendra Prasad, J. B. Kripalani and Mahadev Desai[cite: 3]. Shukla was the person who brought Gandhi there, not the movement's principal leader.
