India Invasion — Background
  • Invited by Daulat Khan Lodi to invade India
  • Learned Tulughma strategy from Uzbeks
  • Ottoman-influenced artillery experts: Ustad Ali Quli and Mustafa Rumi
  • Babur enumerated 7 principal rulers; not an exhaustive list of all Indian states
  • Muslim kingdoms: Bengal, Delhi, Malwa, Bahmani, Gujarat
  • Hindu kingdoms: Mewar (Rana Sanga), Vijayanagara (Krishna Deva Raya)
Babur's 4 Major Battles
Memory Trick — Babur's Battles

Mnemonic: PAN–KHA–CHA–GHA
PAN = First Battle of Panipat (1526) | KHA = Battle of Khanwa (1527) | CHA = Battle of Chanderi (1528) | GHA = Battle of Ghagra (1529)

Battle Year Opponent Result & Key Fact
1st Battle of Panipat April 1526 Ibrahim Lodi Babur wins using Tulughma + Ottoman-influenced artillery (Ustad Ali Quli and Mustafa Rumi). Firearms predated 1526 in India. Nickname: Qalandar.
Battle of Khanwa March 1527 Rana Sanga Babur wins and assumes Ghazi. Renunciation of wine and abolition of Tamgha on Muslims were announced before the battle.
Battle of Chanderi Jan 1528 Medini Rai Babur wins. Rajput women committed Jauhar.
Battle of Ghagra May 1529 Afghans (Mahmud Lodi) Babur wins on land and water, strengthens his eastern position and reaches an agreement with Bengal; not permanent control of all India.

Akbar's Reforms
⚙️ Mansabdari System
  • Decimal-based system
  • High noble rank: 7000 (Man Singh, Mirza Aziz Koka); princes could hold higher ranks
  • Zat = personal rank | Sawar = cavalry
  • 66 theoretical grades in Ain-i-Akbari; not all used in practice
🌾 Land Revenue (Ain-i-Dahsala)
  • Introduced by Todar Mal (1580 CE)
  • Cash rates based on ten-year averages of yields and prices, generally about one-third of produce
  • Taqavi loans — advance to farmers
  • 4 land types: Polaj, Parati, Chachar, Banjar
🕉️ Religious Policies — Sulh-e-Kul (Tolerance)
  • 1562 — prohibited enslavement of war captives and their families
  • 1563 — End of Pilgrimage tax
  • 1564 — Abolition of Jizya
  • 1575 — Ibadat Khana established (Fatehpur Sikri) — closed 1582
  • 1579 — Mahzar: authority to choose among conflicting juristic opinions under stated conditions
  • 1582 — Din-i-Ilahi founded (Tauhid-e-Ilahi per Abul Fazl)
  • Practice of Jharokha Darshan
  • Jain saint Hari Vijay Suri: Title Jagat Guru
  • Jin Chandra Suri: title Yug Pradhan
  • Coins bore depictions of Sita and Ram

⚠️ Exam Traps — Mughal Empire
Trap #1 — Founder vs Consolidator

Babur founded the Mughal state in 1526. Akbar greatly expanded and consolidated its institutions; “real founder” is an evaluative label.

Trap #2 — Baburnama Language

The Baburnama was written in Chagatai Turkish, not Persian or Arabic. Its Persian translator was Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khana, and its English translator was Annette Beveridge.

Trap #3 — Humayun's Death

Humayun died in 1556 CE after falling down the stairs of the library at Dinpanah. He did not die in battle.

Trap #4 — Tulughma and Artillery

At Panipat in 1526, Babur combined Tulughma with artillery against Ibrahim Lodi. Tulughma had an earlier Central Asian history, and firearms had already been used in India.

Trap #5 — Ain-i-Dahsala vs Ain-i-Akbari

Ain-i-Dahsala (1580) was the land-revenue system associated with Todar Mal. Ain-i-Akbari is the third part of Abul Fazl’s Akbarnama. They are entirely different.

Trap #6 — Tansen's Guru

Tansen’s Sufi spiritual guide was Muhammad Ghaus; his traditional musical teacher was Swami Haridas. Akbar patronized him as Miyan Tansen; his original name was Ramtanu Pandey.

Trap #7 — Nur Jahan's Real Name

Nur Jahan was originally Mehrunnisa. Her father, Ghiyas Beg, held the title Itimad-ud-Daula. She commissioned his tomb at Agra, an important early marble-and-inlay monument.

Trap #8 — Who built Purana Qila?

Humayun began Dinpanah at the site; Sher Shah developed and altered the complex now called Purana Qila. Both rulers are associated with it.

Trap #9 — Guru Arjan Dev vs Tegh Bahadur

Jahangir executed the fifth Sikh Guru, Guru Arjan Dev, for supporting Khusrau’s rebellion. Aurangzeb executed the ninth Guru, Guru Tegh Bahadur.

Trap #10 — Din-i-Ilahi vs Ibadat Khana

Ibadat Khana was established in 1575 as a centre for inter-religious dialogue and closed in 1582. Din-i-Ilahi was founded by Akbar in 1582. They were different initiatives from different years.

Trap #11 — Kohinoor Diamond

Nadir Shah carried the Kohinoor from the Mughal treasury in 1739. Its early ownership is uncertain; claims about Malik Kafur and the 1636 treaty should not be treated as secure identifications.

Trap #12 — Dagh and Huliya

Alauddin Khilji originally introduced the Dagh system of branding horses and the Huliya descriptive-roll system. Sher Shah Suri revived them, and Akbar also used them. Alauddin was the original initiator.
