⚠️ Exam Traps
Trap #1 — Vijayanagara Founders

Harihara I and Bukka founded Vijayanagara in 1336 CE. Traditions link them with Kampili, but their precise earlier offices are uncertain.

Trap #2 — Religion: Shaivism vs Vaishnavism

Vijayanagara rulers patronized both Shaiva and Vaishnava institutions. Krishna Deva Raya was a Vaishnava devotee who also supported Shaiva temples; the whole empire had no single fixed state sect.

Trap #3 — Battle of Talikota — Who Fought?

The nominal Vijayanagara ruler was Sadasiva Raya, but actual power was held by his minister Rama Raya. The opposing confederacy comprised four sultanates—Ahmadnagar, Bijapur, Golconda and Bidar—not a single Bahmani state.

Trap #4 — Allasani Peddana's Title

Allasani Peddana is called Andhra Kavita Pitamaha, the “Grandfather of Telugu Poetry”. Krishna Deva Raya is associated with Andhra Bhoja; distinguish the poet’s title from the ruler’s.

Trap #5 — Bahmani Founder's Names

The same Bahmani founder was known as Hasan Gangu, held the title Zafar Khan, and ruled as Alauddin Hasan Bahman Shah. Zafar Khan was a title, not a separate personal original name.

Trap #6 — Capital Shift (Bahmani)

The first Bahmani capital was Gulbarga (Ahsanabad) under the founder Alauddin Hasan. Shihabuddin Ahmad Shah I later shifted the capital to Bidar. Match the capital shift with the correct ruler.

Trap #7 — Hyderabad & Charminar

Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah founded Hyderabad and also built the Charminar. He belonged to the Qutb Shahi dynasty of Golconda, not the Bahmani dynasty.

Trap #8 — Gol Gumbaz Builder

Muhammad Adil Shah built Gol Gumbaz. Ibrahim II wrote Kitab-e-Nauras, while the administrative promotion of Dakhani is associated with Ibrahim I.
