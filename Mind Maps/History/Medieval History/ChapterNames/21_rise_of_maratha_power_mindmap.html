⚠️ Exam Traps
Trap #1 — Shivaji’s Two Coronations

Shivaji’s first coronation on 6 June 1674 was officiated by Gaga Bhatta. The second ceremony on 24 September 1674 was conducted under Nischalpuri.

Trap #2 — Two Separate Treaties of Purandar

Purandar, 1665: Shivaji and Jai Singh; 23 forts ceded, 12 retained. Purandar, 1776: the Peshwa’s government and the Company during Madhavrao II’s minority; separate agreements.

Trap #3 — Chauth vs Sardeshmukhi

Chauth was a one-fourth or 25% levy collected from neighbouring states in return for non-aggression. Sardeshmukhi was a one-tenth or 10% tribute. They were different taxes.

Trap #4 — Mughal Recognition of Shahu’s Claims

The settlement negotiated by Balaji Vishwanath and the Sayyids, confirmed in 1719, obtained Mughal recognition of Shahu’s Swarajya and Chauth/Sardeshmukhi claims in six Deccan Subahs, subject to obligations.

Trap #5 — 3rd Battle of Panipat — Date

The Third Battle of Panipat was fought on 14 January 1761. Ahmad Shah Abdali of Afghanistan, not the Mughals, defeated the Marathas. The Peshwa at the time was Balaji Baji Rao.

Trap #6 — Peshwa vs Ashtapradhan

The Peshwa began as prime minister in the Ashtapradhan. Bhat family succession began with Baji Rao I in 1720; practical authority became predominant under Balaji Baji Rao, especially after Shahu’s death.

Trap #7 — Anglo-Maratha Wars GG

First Anglo-Maratha War = Warren Hastings. Second = Lord Wellesley. Third = Lord Hastings. Warren Hastings and Lord Hastings were different people associated with different wars.
