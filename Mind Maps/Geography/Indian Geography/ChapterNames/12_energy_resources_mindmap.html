📊 Two Types of Energy
🔒 Non-Renewable Resources
  • Finite stocks; not replenished on a human time-scale
  • Examples: Coal, Petroleum, Natural Gas, Thermal, Nuclear, Shale Gas
  • Installed capacity (around July 2026): Fossil-fuel based: ~251.5 GW (~46%)
♻️ Renewable Resources
  • Naturally replenished on a human time-scale
  • Examples: Biogas, Solar, Geo-thermal, Tidal, Hydro
  • Installed capacity (around July 2026): Renewable: 291.73 GW (31 July 2026)

🛢️ Petroleum & Oil Refineries
🛢️ Petroleum — Black Gold
  • India's oldest operating oilfield: Digboi, Assam
  • Early crude pipelines: Digboi field–refinery (1901); major Assam–Guwahati line (1962), extended to Barauni (1964)
  • Major areas: Mumbai High, Ankleshwar (Gujarat), Digboi (Assam)
  • Top producers: Gujarat > Rajasthan > Assam (onshore, 2023–24)
Oil Refineries:
Sector Refineries

Ownership note Bina = BPCL public sector; Bathinda = HMEL joint venture
Private Jamnagar, SEZ Jamnagar, Vadinar
💨 Natural Gas
  • Cleaner-burning fossil fuel; not zero-carbon; can occur with petroleum fields
  • Major reserves: Krishna-Godavari Basin (KG Basin), Gulf of Khambhat
  • Top producers: Assam & Arunachal Pradesh > Rajasthan > Tripura (onshore, 2023–24)
  • HVJ Pipeline: Hazira–Vijaipur–Jagdishpur — Landmark cross-country natural-gas pipeline, commissioned 1987
Shale Gas:
  • Found between sedimentary rocks
  • Abundant in: Cambay, KG, Cauvery, Assam, Ganga and Gondwana/Damodar; Bengal also studied
🚨 EXAM TRAP
  • Barauni oil refinery = Bihar (public sector) — BPSC most asked!
  • Major Assam crude pipeline: Guwahati section 1962, extended to Barauni, Bihar in 1964; Digboi field–refinery line operated from 1901
  • Digboi = India's first oil reserve (Assam)
  • Shale gas occurs within fine-grained sedimentary rocks, not igneous rocks.
  • Onshore natural gas (2023–24): Assam & Arunachal Pradesh group → Rajasthan → Tripura

☢️ Nuclear / Atomic Energy — Uranium: Metal of Hope
☢️ Nuclear Energy — Key Facts
  • Uranium is popularly called “Metal of Hope”
  • Minerals: Uranium, Thorium, Monazite (Kerala), Beryllium
  • Father of Atomic Energy in India: Dr. Homi Jehangir Bhabha
  • Installed capacity: 8.78 GW (July 2026)

☀️ Solar Energy — 3rd Largest Globally
☀️ Solar Energy — Key Facts
  • Type: Renewable energy; very low operational air emissions
  • Source: Sun
  • India = 3rd largest country by installed solar-power capacity in world
  • Landmark single-location solar project: Kamuthi, Tamil Nadu — 648 MW
  • Largest Solar Park: Bhadla Solar Park, Rajasthan

2. Gujarat

📍 Bihar & Jharkhand — Energy Resources
🛢️ Barauni Oil Refinery — Bihar's Most Important
  • Location: Barauni, Bihar
  • Sector: Public Sector
  • Major historic Assam–Guwahati crude line (1962) was extended to Barauni in 1964; Digboi field–refinery line dates to 1901
  • Connected to Assam's Naharkatiya oil fields
  • This is one of the most frequently tested Bihar energy facts in BPSC examinations.
🖤 Coal — Jharkhand's Crown
  • Jharkhand = #2 geological coal resources (1 Apr 2025), after Odisha
  • Jharia = principal coking-coal field coal region in India
  • Major coal areas: Jharia, Bokaro, Karnpura, Chandrapura, Giridih, Rajmahal
  • Gondwana coal = 98% of India's coal production
  • Damodar Valley = Gondwana coal belt = JH + WB
🏭 Thermal & Power Plants near Bihar-JH
  • DVC Thermal Plants: Bokaro, Chandrapura, Koderma (Jharkhand)
  • Coal → Thermal Power → supplied to Bihar + WB region
  • Raniganj coal belt (WB) also near Bihar-JH corridor
💧 Hydro — Tilaiya (Jharkhand)
  • Tilaiya Dam Hydel Power Station — Jharkhand
  • Barakar River — part of Damodar Valley
  • Also: DVC dams generate hydroelectricity
💨 Shale Gas — Damodar Basin
  • Shale gas deposits in Damodar Basin (JH + WB)
  • Also in: Bengal Basin, Vindhya Basin
  • Future energy potential for Jharkhand
⚡ BPSC MASTER RECALL — Bihar/JH Energy
  • Bihar = Barauni Oil Refinery (public sector); major Assam crude pipeline extension reached Barauni in 1964
  • Jharkhand = #2 geological coal resources after Odisha (1 Apr 2025)
  • Jharia = principal coking-coal field coal region (Jharkhand)
  • Tilaiya = hydro project, Jharkhand
  • DVC thermal plants: Bokaro, Chandrapura, Koderma (JH)
  • Shale gas = Damodar Basin (JH + WB)
🚨 EXAM TRAP
  • Barauni = Bihar (public sector refinery) — NOT Jharkhand
  • Historic major Assam–Guwahati–Barauni crude system: Guwahati section 1962; Barauni extension 1964
  • Geological coal resources #1 = Odisha (1 Apr 2025) | Production #1 = Odisha (2024–25)
  • Tilaiya Dam in Jharkhand is a hydroelectric project on the Barakar River and is frequently tested.
