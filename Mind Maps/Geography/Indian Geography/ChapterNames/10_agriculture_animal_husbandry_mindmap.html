🌾 Major Food Crops — Key Facts
🍚 Rice
  • Origin: South and East Asia
  • Climate: Tropical Humid
  • Temp: About 20–27°C
  • Rainfall: About 100–200 cm, or adequate irrigation
  • Soil: Loamy + Alluvial
  • Varieties: Aus, Aman, Boro
🌾 Wheat
  • Origin: Fertile Crescent / Southwest Asia
  • Climate: Temperate
  • Temp: 15–25°C
  • Rainfall: 50–75 cm
  • Soil: Loamy / Alluvial
🌽 Bajra (Millet)
  • Origin: Africa
  • Climate: Sub-tropical
  • Temp: 30–35°C
  • Rainfall: 50–75 cm
  • Soil: Sandy soil
🌾 Jowar
  • Jowar — origin: Northeastern Africa
  • Climate: Sub-tropical
  • Temp: 27–32°C
  • Rainfall: 30–100 cm
  • Soil: Loamy
🌿 Barley
  • Barley — origin: Fertile Crescent / Southwest Asia
  • Climate: Temperate
  • Temp: 10–18°C
  • Rainfall: 50–75 cm
  • Soil: Sandy
🌽 Maize
  • Origin: Mexico / Mesoamerica
  • Climate: Sub-tropical
  • Temp: 21–27°C
  • Rainfall: 60–120 cm
  • Soil: Loamy

🚨 EXAM TRAP
  • Rice origin = South and East Asia | Wheat origin = Fertile Crescent / Southwest Asia
  • Rabi = sown Oct-Nov, harvest Apr-May → winter crops (wheat, barley)
  • Kharif = sown Jun-Jul → monsoon crops (rice, cotton)
  • Jowar + Bajra + Ragi = Coarse grains / Millets; Ragi = Finger Millet
  • Wheat disease = Rust (Ratuwa) | Rice disease = Blast + Khaira
☕ Major Cash Crops
🌿 Cotton — White Gold
  • Soil: Black Soil
  • Requires: 210 frost-free days
  • India: 1st in cotton area; 2nd in production
  • Called: White Gold (white cotton)
🌿 Jute — Golden Fibre
  • Called: Golden Fibre
  • Grows in: alluvial soil + humid climate
  • India = major producer (WB, Bihar, Assam)
🍵 Tea
  • Soil: Acidic soil
  • World #1: China
  • India #1: Assam
  • Special type: Oolong tea
☕ Coffee
  • Soil: Laterite soil
  • World #1: Brazil
  • India #1: Karnataka
  • Varieties: Arabica, Liberica, Robusta

  • India has one of the world's largest irrigated areas; 56.37% of Net Area Sown was net irrigated in 2022–23
  • Most irrigation by pond → Tamil Nadu
  • Drip irrigation delivers water near plant roots through pipes and emitters
  • Longest irrigation canal → Indira Gandhi Canal (Satluj River)
  • National Water Resource Council (1983) → Chairman: Prime Minister
🚨 EXAM TRAP
  • Cotton = White Gold; Jute = Golden Fibre.
  • Tea world #1 = China | Coffee world #1 = Brazil
  • Tea soil = Acidic | Coffee soil = Laterite
  • Major irrigation source = Well & Tubewells (62.80%) — NOT canals
  • Most pond irrigation = Tamil Nadu

⚡ BPSC RECALL — Bihar Animal Husbandry
  • Bihar = 10.67% livestock growth (5th among the selected major positive-growth states (2012–2019))
  • Bihar = major cattle + buffalo state in Ganga plains
  • Jute also grown in Bihar (along with WB and Assam)
  • Milk: UP → RJ → MP (Bihar not in top 3 but significant)
  • Bihar = good for fish (rivers: Ganga, Kosi, Gandak)
🚨 EXAM TRAP
  • India = 1st in milk production (~25% world share, 2024–25)
  • Top milk state = UP (NOT RJ or Punjab)
  • Top fish state = Andhra Pradesh (NOT Kerala or WB)
  • Buffalo gestation = 310 days (longest) | Pig = 114 days (shortest)
  • Livestock largest % = Cattle (36.04%) then Goat (27.74%) then Buffalo (20.47%)
