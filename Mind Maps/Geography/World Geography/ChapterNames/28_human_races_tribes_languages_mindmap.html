🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Historical Racial Classifications

Historical term: “Caucasoid”

Obsolete classification used in older textbooks for peoples of Europe, West Asia and North Africa; not a valid biological race.

Historical term: “Mongoloid”

Obsolete classification used in older texts for East/Central Asian and some Indigenous American peoples; not a valid biological race.

Historical term: “Negroid”

Obsolete classification used in older texts for sub-Saharan African peoples; not a valid biological race. Do not extend this label to Oceania.

⚡ Quick Recall
Human variation is continuous and overlapping. Learn these labels only as obsolete historical classifications; modern anthropology does not divide humanity into three discrete biological races.

🏠 Dwellings of Tribal and Nomadic People
DwellingDetails
YurtPortable round trellis-frame dwelling, traditionally felt-covered; Central/Inner Asian steppes.
KraalSouthern African livestock enclosure; historically also an enclosed homestead or settlement.
IglooShelter of compacted snow blocks, traditionally used by some Inuit in Arctic Canada and Greenland.
Aul (aoul)Village or settlement, sometimes historically fortified; Caucasus and parts of Central Asia.
⚡ Quick Recall
Igloo = snow shelter; yurt = felt-covered portable dwelling; kraal = livestock enclosure/homestead; aul = village or settlement.
