Statement : In order to replenish the nutrients in the soil, it is important to grow different types of crops in every alternate season.
Assumptions :
(I) A crop can never be grown for the second time in the same field.
(II)If a different crop is grown in the successive season, no additional nutrients such as fertilizers are required to be added to the soil at all.

(I) और
(II) दी गई हैं। सही विकल्प चुनिए—
कथन: मिट्टी में पोषक तत्त्वों की पुनःपूर्ति के लिए प्रत्येक वैकल्पिक मौसम में अलग-अलग प्रकार की फसलें उगाना महत्वपूर्ण है।
मान्यताएँ:
(I) एक फसल को उसी खेत में दूसरी बार कभी नहीं उगाया जा सकता।

Which is not an area of Mediterranean type of Agriculture?

✅ Correct Answer: (C)
  • The Mediterranean climate is found between 30° -40° /45° latitudes on both sides of the equator, primarily on the western parts of continents.
  • This climatic region is found around the Mediterranean Sea, including Spain, Portugal, France, Southern parts of Italy, Greece, western Turkiye, Syria, western Israel and northern Africa.
  • It is also present in California (North America), central Chile (South America), southwestern and southern Australia and the south Western Part of South Africa.
