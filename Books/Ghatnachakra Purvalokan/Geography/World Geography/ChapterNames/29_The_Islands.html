Which of the following is the correct sequence of the Islands of Indonesia from West to East?
इंडोनेशिया के द्वीपों का पश्चिम से पूर्व की ओर सही क्रम कौन-सा है?
✅ Correct Answer: (B)
  • The correct west-to-east sequence is: Sumatra → Java → Bali → Lombok.
  • Indonesia's islands can be grouped into the Greater Sunda Islands, the Lesser Sunda Islands (Nusa Tenggara), the Moluccas (Maluku), and the western part of New Guinea generally known as Papua.
  • The capital, Jakarta, is located near the north-western coast of Java.
  • पश्चिम से पूर्व की ओर सही क्रम है—सुमात्रा → जावा → बाली → लोम्बोक।
