Reason (R): The basic needs of people vary according to regions and time.

  • Thus, Reason (R) is also correct.

In which of the following years did the United Nations adopt the definition of 'Absolute poverty'?
निम्नलिखित में से किस वर्ष में संयुक्त राष्ट्र संघ ने 'परम गरीबी' की परिभाषा अंगीकृत की थी?
✅ Correct Answer: (B)
  • Explanation: In 1995, at the 'World Summit for Social Development' held in Copenhagen (Denmark), the United Nations defined poverty in two forms: Absolute poverty and Overall poverty.
  • The condition of being severely deprived of basic human needs such as food, safe drinking water, sanitation facilities, health, shelter, education, and access to information is defined as 'Absolute poverty'.
  • Additionally, conditions such as malnutrition, temporary and inadequate livelihood, morbidity, living in a polluted environment, etc., which adversely affect decision-making capacity and a dignified civil, social, and cultural life, are defined as Overall poverty.
  • वर्ष 1995 में कोपेनहेगन (डेनमार्क) में आयोजित 'सामाजिक विकास के लिए विश्व शिखर सम्मेलन' में संयुक्त राष्ट्र ने गरीबी को परम गरीबी (Absolute poverty) एवं समग्र गरीबी (Overall poverty) के दो रूपों में परिभाषित किया।
  • मूलभूत मानवीय आवश्यकताओं जैसे भोजन, सुरक्षित पेयजल, स्वच्छता सुविधाएं, स्वास्थ्य, आवास, शिक्षा तथा सूचना तक पहुंच से गंभीर रूप से वंचित रहने की स्थिति को 'परम गरीबी' (Absolute Poverty) के रूप में परिभाषित किया गया है।
  • इसके अतिरिक्त कुपोषण, अस्थायी एवं अपर्याप्त आजीविका, बीमारी की स्थिति, दूषित वातावरण में निवास आदि जैसी स्थितियों में होने, जिनसे निर्णयन क्षमता तथा सम्मानजनक नागरिक, सामाजिक एवं सांस्कृतिक जीवन पर दुष्प्रभाव पड़ता है, को समग्र गरीबी के रूप में परिभाषित किया गया है।

Which of the following committees determined the poverty line based solely on nutritional requirements?

✅ Correct Answer: (A)
  • Explanation: In 1977, a Task Force on 'Minimum Needs Programme and Effective Consumption Demand' was constituted under the chairmanship of Dr. Y.K. Alagh, which submitted its report in 1979.
  • This task force determined the poverty line based solely on nutritional requirements.
  • Keeping in mind the 'minimum nutritional requirement' report, the task force made the minimum calorie requirement the basis for determining the poverty line.
  • According to this, 2435 calories (approx. 2400) per capita per day for rural areas and 2095 calories (approx. 2100) per capita per day for urban areas are required.
  • Those whose expenditure is less than the fulfillment of calorie requirements will be below the poverty line.
  • On this standard, at 1973-74 prices, the poverty line was determined at Rs 49.09 per capita per month for rural areas and Rs 56.64 per capita per month for urban areas.
  • In 1989, an expert group was constituted under the chairmanship of Prof. D.T. Lakdawala by the then Planning Commission, which submitted its report in July 1993.
  • In December 2005, the then Planning Commission constituted an expert group under the chairmanship of Suresh D. Tendulkar, which submitted its report in November 2009.
  • The Rangarajan Committee was constituted in 2012 by the then Planning Commission to review and amend the Tendulkar Committee's report.
  • This committee submitted its report to the Commission in June 2014.
  • वर्ष 1977 में डॉ. वाई. के. अलघ की अध्यक्षता में 'न्यूनतम आवश्यकता कार्यक्रम और प्रभावी उपभोग मांग' पर एक कार्यदल (Task Force) का गठन किया गया जिसने अपनी रिपोर्ट वर्ष 1979 में प्रस्तुत की।

✅ Correct Answer: (D)
  • Explanation: The Lorenz Curve was propounded by Dr. Max O' Lorenz to study the inequality of wealth and income.
  • It is called the 'Lorenz curve' after him.
  • This curve is also used to study the distribution of income, wealth, wages, profit, capital, and production, etc.
  • The 'Lorenz curve' is a graphical measure of the deviation of the actual distribution from the line of equal distribution.
  • The closer it is to the Line of Equal Distribution, the lower the amount of dispersion (or inequality of income) in that series, meaning fewer inequalities will be found in the distribution.
  • Conversely, the farther the Lorenz curve is from the line of equal distribution, the higher the amount of dispersion in the series.
  • If the Lorenz curve falls on the line of equal distribution, dispersion is considered non-existent in the series.
  • लॉरेंज वक्र (Lorenz Curve) का प्रतिपादन डॉ. मैक्स ओ' लॉरेंज (Dr. Max O' Lorenz) ने धन और आय की विषमता का अध्ययन करने के लिए किया था।

  • In this index, countries are divided into five parts on the basis of hunger status, namely low (<9.9 score), moderate (10.0-19.9 score), serious (20.0-34.9 score), alarming (35.0-49.9 score) and extremely alarming (> 50 score).
  • 'वैश्विक भूख सूचकांक' (Global Hunger Index) विश्व के विकासशील देशों में भुखमरी तथा कुपोषण की गणना एवं इसके तुलनात्मक अध्ययन हेतु एक बहु-आयामी सूचकांक है।
