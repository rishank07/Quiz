Which of the following subjects is related to the study of important statistics concerning population and human communities?

✅ Correct Answer: (C)
  • Demography is the study of all the determinants of population and human communities and their consequences.
  • It includes the study of population size, regional distribution and composition, changes occurring in them, and the causes responsible for such changes, such as birth rate, death rate, migration and social mobility.
  • Thus, both the qualitative and quantitative aspects of population are studied and analysed under demography.
  • जनांकिकी (Demography) के अंतर्गत जनसंख्या एवं मानव समुदायों के समस्त निर्धारक तत्वों तथा उनके परिणामों का अध्ययन किया जाता है।
