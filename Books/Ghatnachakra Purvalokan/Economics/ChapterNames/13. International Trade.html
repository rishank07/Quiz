कूट :
✅ Correct Answer: (C)
  • The sum of imports and exports of visible and invisible items in a financial year is called 'Current Account'.
  • If in a financial year, the import of visible and invisible items is more than the export of visible and invisible items, then that situation is called 'Current Account Deficit' (CAD).
  • Current account deficit = Import of visible and invisible items - Export of visible and invisible items, or in other words, it can be said that "when a country's payments exceed its receipts, then it is called Current Account Deficit." Hence assertion (A) is true, but reason (R) is false.
  • एक वित्तीय वर्ष में दृश्य और अदृश्य मदों के आयात तथा निर्यात का योग ही 'चालू खाता' (Current Account) कहलाता है।
